# The True Cost of E-Commerce App Development in Mumbai (2026)

Mumbai is the financial capital of India, and the e-commerce sector here is booming. If you are a retailer, wholesaler, or startup looking to transition online, your first question is likely: **"How much does it cost to build an e-commerce app?"**

In this guide, we break down the real costs of e-commerce development in Mumbai and what factors influence the final price.

## 1. Standard Template vs. Custom Build

The biggest factor in pricing is whether you use a pre-built template (like Shopify or WooCommerce) or build a custom solution from scratch (using React Native, Next.js, and Node.js).

### Template-Based E-Commerce (Shopify/WooCommerce)
- **Best for:** Small businesses testing the waters with a limited product catalog.
- **Estimated Cost:** ₹50,000 - ₹1,50,000
- **Pros:** Fast to launch, lower initial cost.
- **Cons:** High monthly subscription fees, limited scalability, difficult to implement complex custom features, and generic design.

### Custom E-Commerce Application
- **Best for:** Serious startups and established brands expecting high traffic and needing a unique brand experience.
- **Estimated Cost:** ₹3,00,000 - ₹10,00,000+
- **Pros:** Total ownership of the code, unlimited scalability, no monthly platform fees, completely custom UI/UX.
- **Cons:** Higher upfront investment and longer development timeline (2-4 months).

## 2. Essential Features that Drive Cost

When building a custom e-commerce app, specific features will dictate the budget:

*   **Payment Gateway Integration:** Integrating Razorpay, Stripe, or Paytm requires secure backend architecture.
*   **Real-Time Inventory Management:** Syncing your app with your physical warehouse or ERP software is complex but necessary for growing businesses.
*   **AI Recommendations:** "Products you may also like" algorithms require advanced machine learning integrations.
*   **Delivery Tracking:** Integrating with Delhivery, Shiprocket, or Dunzo APIs for live tracking adds development hours.

## 3. The "Hidden" Costs of App Development

Many business owners forget to budget for post-launch expenses:

*   **Server Hosting:** AWS, Google Cloud, or Vercel (₹2,000 - ₹10,000/month depending on traffic).
*   **Apple & Google Developer Fees:** Apple charges $99/year, and Google charges a one-time $25 fee to list your app.
*   **Maintenance & Bug Fixes:** Technology constantly evolves. Expect to spend 15-20% of your initial development cost annually on maintenance.
